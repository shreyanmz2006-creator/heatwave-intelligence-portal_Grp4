Severe Heatwave Alert Bulletin

⚠ SEVERE HEATWAVE WARNING ACTIVE — Vidarbha & Marathwada Zone

Alert: Severe Heatwave Warning issued for Region: Vidarbha & Marathwada Zone

Maximum recorded temperature: 47°C, exceeding normal by 6°C. Bulletin Version: 2.1, issued by AWS network in coordination with the IMD.

This bulletin classifies the current event as a HW, escalating in select districts to a SHW condition. Citizens are advised to avoid outdoor exposure between 12 PM and 4 PM, stay hydrated, and follow local advisory updates issued by disaster-management authorities.

Vulnerable groups – children, the elderly, and outdoor workers – should take extra precautionary measures during this period.
